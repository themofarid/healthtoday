# "My experience" template

Use this after you have really tried TonicGreens. Write only what you actually did and noticed.

## Where it goes
In `drafts/tonicgreens.md` (or `approved/tonicgreens.md` if already approved), put it in the intro,
right after the **How do you take it?** paragraph, starting with a bold line exactly like this:

**My experience with TonicGreens**

Then fill in your own words, for example:

- I ordered the <pack size> in <month year> and used it for <how long>.
- How I prepared it: <water, a smoothie, what I mixed it with>.
- Taste and texture: <your honest words, including what you did not like>.
- How it fit into my day: <how easy or hard it was to keep up>.
- Delivery and customer service, if you dealt with them: <what happened>.
- Closing line: This is only my personal experience, and yours may be different.

## Rules that keep the page safe
- Describe your experience, not medical results. Do not mention conditions, diagnoses, treatment, or promises such as "it fixed my...".
- Keep a mix of what you liked and did not like. It reads as honest and it is more convincing.
- Add a photo of your own pack or glass (see the Images section of the README): `![My TonicGreens pack](images/my-tonicgreens-pack.jpg)`.
- Then delete or change the sentence in the intro that says "I have not taken it myself", because it will no longer be true.
- Run `python -m tools build`: the claim checker reads your new text too and will flag risky wording.
